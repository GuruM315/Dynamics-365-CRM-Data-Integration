# Dashboard Catalogue

This catalogue describes proposed dashboard themes for a CRM analytics portfolio. Include only pages and KPIs that exist in the actual solution or that you have independently built as a demonstration.

## 1. Management dashboard

**Purpose:** Provide leadership with a consolidated view of commercial performance.

Potential pages:
- Executive overview
- Revenue and invoicing trends
- Opportunity pipeline and stage distribution
- Target versus actual performance
- Regional and business-unit comparisons
- Salesperson performance overview

Potential KPIs (subject to business definitions and available data):
- Invoice amount
- Open pipeline value
- Won opportunity value
- Target achievement percentage
- Number of active opportunities
- Period-over-period change

## 2. Sales dashboard

**Purpose:** Help sales leaders and account executives monitor execution and follow-up.

Potential pages:
- Salesperson performance
- Account and contact overview
- Opportunity funnel and ageing
- Pipeline by stage, region and owner
- Invoice performance
- Target versus actual

Potential analysis:
- Opportunity count and value by stage.
- Pipeline by owner and expected close period.
- Invoices by salesperson and period.
- Account activity and opportunity coverage.

## 3. Marketing dashboard

**Purpose:** Evaluate marketing activity and its contribution to demand generation, where relevant CRM and campaign data is available.

Potential pages:
- Campaign overview
- Lead volume and source
- Lead status and qualification
- Campaign-to-opportunity conversion
- Campaign contribution by region or business unit
- Engagement trend, if engagement data is integrated

Potential KPIs:
- Leads generated
- Qualified leads
- Lead-to-opportunity conversion rate
- Campaign-sourced pipeline
- Campaign cost and ROI only if reliable cost and attribution data are available

## 4. Page design standards

- Keep KPI definitions consistent across audiences.
- Show reporting period, currency and refresh timestamp.
- Use accessible contrast, clear labels and consistent number formats.
- Provide drill-through only where it supports a clear business task.
- Avoid exposing personally identifiable or commercially sensitive detail to unauthorized viewers.
